Journey

I'm Kevin Ahiavee, a Beninese entrepreneur. I turned my passion for digital into an agency, then into an ecosystem of companies that strengthen one another.

Long known online as the “Prince du Digital”, I now run KK SMART COM, grow KOKA MOBILITY and its startup Chic Tuk Tuk, and lead La Marque du Battant. My common thread: creating value here, with local teams, for an audience beyond our borders.

My philosophy

It all starts with a connection…

  • Relationship before transaction

    A client, a partner, a team member: everything starts with a genuine relationship. Business follows once trust is there.

  • A go-getter once, a go-getter always

    Hope, determination and pushing your limits are not slogans. They are work habits, especially on the hard days.

  • Grow with those who build

    A company moves forward when its team moves forward with it. I open my projects to partners and I want my team to share in the success.

  • Think continental, act local

    Every project starts from a concrete need I see in Cotonou, and aims from day one at an audience beyond our borders.

Pan-African vision

An Africa that tells its own story, moves and defines its style on its own terms.

My companies meet three needs of the continent: telling our brands' stories in our own codes, making urban mobility more accessible and giving the continent's builders, entrepreneurs and professionals in every sector, a lifestyle that reflects them.

Benin is my starting point, not my limit. I am looking for partners, talent and investors who share this ambition.
